How I work

A simple process, from first idea to something people can use.

  1. 1

    Understand

    I start with who the app is for and what it needs to do, before I write any code.

  2. 2

    Design

    I sketch the flow and design the screens phone-sized first, so it feels right in the hand.

  3. 3

    Build

    I build in small steps with clean, readable code that is easy to maintain.

  4. 4

    Improve

    I share it, take feedback, and keep refining until it works well.
